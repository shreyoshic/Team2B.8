Site Example
A public test website. Use it to test scrapers, SEO tools, link checkers, form handlers, and anything else that interacts with web pages.
All email addresses on this site are dummy and none of them are monitored.
Recommended for QA automation & testing.
This site is intentionally published as a stable, public example for QA automation,
      end-to-end and integration test suites, web-scraping practice, and demos. You are welcome
      to point automated tests, crawlers, and coding-assistant examples at these pages and their
      form/table/link/media/file/API endpoints.
